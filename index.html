Questions before you book

The things founders ask us first. Anything else, bring it to the call.

  • Your content stays yours.
  • Access you control.
  • Based in Germany, built for GDPR.
Is the content written by AI?

The first draft is. It is written by our system from a knowledge base about your product, not from a model's general knowledge. Then an editor checks every claim against your product docs and fixes tone, structure and SEO, and you approve it. Nothing is published without a person signing it off.

Will Google penalise AI-assisted content?

Google ranks content on whether it is helpful, not on how it was produced. What gets demoted is thin, unchecked content published at volume. Every article here is grounded in your product, fact-checked by an editor and approved by you before it goes live.

Who owns the content and the system?

You do. The articles, the knowledge base about your product, your brand voice guide and the workflows built for you stay yours, including if you stop working with us.

What do you need from our side?

Access to Google Search Console, GA4, your CMS and the social accounts we post to. One person who approves content within the agreed window, for example two working days. And a product expert we can talk to for 15 minutes, twice a month.

How soon will we see results?

Onboarding takes about six weeks, so the first production articles go live in month two. Search results usually take around three months to show, which is why the minimum term is three months. Every monthly review compares results with the baseline audit from week one.

What does visibility in AI answers mean?

Tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity answer questions by quoting sources. We pick the questions your buyers ask, structure your content so it can be quoted (direct answers, clean headings, schema, data), and check every month whether you are cited for them.

Can we stop after the minimum term?

Yes. After the first three months the program runs month to month, and you can stop at the end of any month. You keep everything that was built.
